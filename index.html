Four key ideas

🔌

Move heat

ΔT = q·X / λ

Heat flowing steadily through a plate needs a temperature drop. High conductivity λ makes the drop small.

Decision: screen out electrical conductors, then rank on λ → aluminum nitride under power chips.

✋

Feel heat

pull ∝ √(λρCp)

You feel the contact temperature. Materials that conduct well and store a lot of heat drag your skin to their own temperature.

Decision: wooden sauna benches. The same group, λ/a½, picks a heat-storing sun wall.

☕

Delay heat

t ≈ x² / 2a

Diffusivity a = λ/ρCp sets how fast a temperature change creeps through a wall. Twice as thick, four times as long.

Decision: for a short trip, lowest a (solid rubber); for months, lowest λ (foam).

🏆

Choose

goal → index

One λ–a chart, four goals: min λ (months), min a (an hour), max λ/a½ (sun wall), max a½/λ (kiln).

Decisions: foam, rubber, concrete, refractory brick.
